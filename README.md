# SOC 2 Control Testing and Audit Readiness: Case Study

> **Fictional case study.** Cedarpoint Analytics is an invented company. Nothing here is real client work, real audit evidence or a real SOC 2 report. This is a design and practice exercise to show how I approach control testing.

## What this is

A worked example of preparing a company for a SOC 2 Type II audit from the GRC analyst side: planning the testing, requesting evidence, testing controls, recording results and reporting what is not ready yet.

**Fictional company:** Cedarpoint Analytics, a Canadian B2B SaaS company with about 120 employees, hosted on a major cloud provider, using a mix of SaaS tools for identity, ticketing and code management.

**Scope:** Trust Services Criteria for Security (Common Criteria), Availability and Confidentiality.

## How I approach control testing

- Test whether a control is designed well and whether it operated through the whole period, not just whether a policy exists.
- Record exactly what was tested, how many items were sampled and why.
- Separate a one-off exception from a pattern, and rate each finding by impact.
- Give every gap an owner and a date.
